Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Adam Lorne Bean (ABN 60 897 757 298) ("we", "us") collects through floodlit.live, how we use it, and your rights. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Where they apply to you, we also follow the EU and UK GDPR.

What we collect

  • When you message us. If you book through WhatsApp or contact us, we receive your name, phone number, the business you work for, and anything you choose to tell us.
  • When you book a session. Your name, email, company, role and payment details. Payments are handled by our payment provider. We do not store card numbers.
  • Project files you share. In a Floodlit session you may share a schedule file (for example a P6 .xer) or project data. See "Your project data" below.
  • Technical data. This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. To display the page, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. These providers receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web requests.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and arrange your session.
  • To deliver the session and send you the materials that come with it.
  • To invoice you and keep business records required by law.
  • To send you information about our programs, only if you have agreed to it. You can opt out at any time.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your project data

Schedule files and project data you share in a session are used only to run that session for you. They are processed using Anthropic's Claude. We do not publish your project data, use it in our marketing, or share it with anyone outside the session without your written permission. Tell us if you want us to delete it after the session, and we will.

Before sharing a file, check you are allowed to share it under your employer's or client's confidentiality obligations.

Who we share it with

We only share personal information with services we use to run the business:

  • Meta (WhatsApp), for messages.
  • Skool, for Catalyst Academy membership.
  • Zoom or Microsoft Teams, for live sessions.
  • Anthropic (Claude), for session analysis.
  • Our payment provider.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We also disclose information where the law requires it.

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we delete or de-identify it when we no longer need it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Contact us using the details below.

Complaints

Contact us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au, or to your local data protection authority.
