Inside the WAR Room

The tools. Built on real jobs.

Each one answers a question your monthly report doesn't. This is the WAR Room I run. The daily prompt is where yours starts.

On the wall

Down and Dirty TAKT

Stand up. Don't sit down. The top 10 tasks on the board, boxes marked, name tags on the crews. The whole scope planned in 15 minutes a day.

Planning doesn't have to take hours. It has to happen every day, in front of the people doing the work.

One number

The Chaos Index

0 to 100, read straight off the schedule. Three parts: pace, overrun and late starts.

Pace 20 · Overrun 65 · Late starts 15

Pace can read fine the whole way through a late job. Overrun is the part that moves your finish date.

Finish date

The Monte Carlo

Thousands of runs through your schedule logic. Realistic productivity, not the planner's best day. A disruption ladder from 0 to 60%.

You get a P50 and a P90 finish. Not one hopeful date.

Devil's Advocate

The Board Assessment

Five advisors read the same schedule. Independently. Then the read is synthesised into what all five agree on.

Keith Cunningham · Thinking Time Eli Goldratt · Constraints Jason Schroeder · Lean Christian Boucousis · Afterburner Alan Bernard · Critical Chain

One question: what don't I see? Not one opinion dressed up as five.

Piping progress

Weighted Welding Dia Inches

Not every weld is equal. A 24 inch butt weld is not a 2 inch one. Count welds and the job looks further along than it is.

Butts and diameter inches, compared between two register dates.

Weld count tells you activity. Diameter inches tell you progress.

Flow

PVR: Project Velocity Ratio

Completed, divided by in progress plus stuck. Above 1.0, work is finishing faster than it piles up. Below 0.65, it's piling up.

Starting work feels like progress. Finishing it is progress.

The real risk

The Earned Value Gap

Planned to date, minus earned. Plotted every day next to the Chaos Index, so the gap between "P6 says fine" and the real position is on one chart.

This is the whiteboard number, grown up.

Dashboard

The 5D Model

Your project in 3D, played week by week. Time and cost on top of the geometry. Every tab built for your job, not a template.

3D buildMaterialsSupply chain mapThroughputTAKT boardZone mapUnassigned and checks

The site team and the PD look at the same picture. Same day.

Every Friday

The Weekly Report

Built from Friday's .xer. The chart leads, then 3 to 5 bullets. Every figure traced to a source and a date. A 7-day look ahead. Nothing repeated from last week without a reason.

Exec level. No waffle. The report that says what the whiteboard says.
